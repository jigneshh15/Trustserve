# TrustServe Investor Pitch — 10–12 Slide Outline

1. Title: TrustServe — Trusted Services. Verified Professionals.
2. Problem: fragmented discovery, trust concerns, unclear pricing.
3. Target customer: housing societies, households and local service professionals.
4. Solution: one platform for booking verified local services.
5. Product demo: service browse → estimate → booking → SafeVisit.
6. Innovation: society group booking, fair pricing, SafeVisit, skill verification, fair earning.
7. Market entry: pilot in one locality and society partnerships; validate demand.
8. Business model: booking commission, optional pro subscription, society partnerships.
9. Competition: Urban Company, instant-service platforms and local contacts; differentiate on society group booking and local trust.
10. Go-to-market: society champions, referral loops, local professional onboarding.
11. Technology and roadmap: Java API, cloud deployment, testing, security improvements.
12. Ask / use of funds: product, verification operations, local launch and support. Add researched figures before presenting.
